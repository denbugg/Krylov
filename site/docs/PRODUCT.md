# ELITE product brief

ELITE is a conversion-first local landing for a single rhythmic-gymnastics hall. Main audience: parents of girls 3–5; a planned 6–10 group can collect interest. Trial: free. Monthly price currently represented as 8,000 RUB.

Primary conversion: enrollment/contact. Preferred contact paths: Telegram, MAX, phone. Phone field should use `type=tel`, `autocomplete=tel`, `inputmode=tel`. Child age is not required. Name optional.

No camps, tournaments, seasons, multi-hall features, or inflated program catalog. No standalone “team of trainers” section.

Polechka is an engagement/retention ecosystem: cartoon, home warm-up, games/cards, stickers, home activity. Do not lead with “Gimlandia” as a separate theme-park concept; it is a secondary world/interface detail.

Parent decision needs to close: proximity/location, age, schedule, safety, coach interaction, preparation, format, cost, what to bring, trial, social environment/common goals/healthy motivation without guarantees.
